# Contribution

If you're enthusiastic about contributing and possess the expertise to craft an adapter for other languages and frameworks (e.g. **Django**, **Rails**, or others), we'd love to hear from you. Your support in extending _SimpleStats_' reach and functionality is invaluable. Feel free to **reach out** to us if you're interested in making a significant impact. Together, we can make _SimpleStats_ even more versatile and accessible to developers across different platforms.
