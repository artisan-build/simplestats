# Contribution

_SimpleStats_ is open source, and contributions of any size are welcome.

## The app

The SimpleStats app lives at [github.com/simplestats-io/app](https://github.com/simplestats-io/app). Found a bug or have an idea for a feature? Open an [issue](https://github.com/simplestats-io/app/issues) or send a pull request. Please read the [contribution guide](https://github.com/simplestats-io/app/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) first, especially before you start on a larger change.

## Clients and integrations

The [Laravel client](https://github.com/simplestats-io/laravel-client), the [PHP client](https://github.com/simplestats-io/php-client), the [Filament plugin](https://github.com/simplestats-io/filament-plugin) and the [Statamic addon](https://github.com/simplestats-io/statamic-addon) are open source as well.

If you want to build an adapter for other languages and frameworks (e.g. **Django**, **Rails**, or others), we'd love to hear from you. Open an issue on the [app repository](https://github.com/simplestats-io/app/issues) and tell us about your plans.
